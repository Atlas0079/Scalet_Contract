# the Blue Sleeves

The Blue Sleeves are an armed group on Alybe built around the remaining Valhalla garrison, led by Major Marek Vik. Its members wear blue armbands and control a district connecting the armory, the repair shops, and the residential areas.

## Formation

After the Blackout, with the former garrison command out of contact, Marek used the warehouses, vehicles, and troop rosters under his control to reassemble the garrison troops, facility guards, and logistics personnel who had lost their units, and incorporated civilian volunteers. The blue armband was first used to identify personnel returning to the ranks, and later extended to volunteers accepted into the organization; this is how residents came to call them the Blue Sleeves.

## Territory and counteroffensive preparations

The armory standardizes equipment models according to the spare parts available, and bodies beyond repair are stripped for parts. The troops treat the restoration of supply lines as preparation for the counteroffensive, holding their territory through escort, road contention, and materiel recovery.

The Blue Sleeves take in civilians and send troops to rescue trapped people; residents entering the territory must register their identities and accept rationing, labor assignments, and requisitioning of supplies. Supply convoys travel back and forth along controlled routes, and accompanying residents must register their destinations and the supplies they carry.

## Differences with the Sole Democratic Party

Marek demands unified wartime command, putting the restoration of order before the residents' political demands. The Sole Democratic Party demands that the city's defense ultimately answer to an elected government. The two sides compete for the same residents, warehouses, and public facilities.
