# the Black Stack

The Black Stack is an armed plunder organization on Alybe that grew out of the port smuggling network, headed by Xie Mi, nicknamed "the Treasurer." They occupy freight yards, warehouses, and nearby routes, managing their members under a fixed organization and rank system.

The pre-Blackout smuggling network already had warehouses, cargo middlemen, and permanent armed escorts. After the Blackout, Xie Mi used food and transport to absorb the escort teams, requiring all seizures to be deposited centrally and supplies then distributed by rank.

## Passage and plunder

The Black Stack sells passage and escort to convoys, detains those who refuse to pay, and seizes other groups' cargo. They honor part of their deals, letting merchants and survivors keep passing through the controlled area, and they also deliberately create situations in which the other side must buy escort or passage.

Within the organization, pocketing seizures is forbidden, and captives who can fetch a ransom may not be killed without permission; residents with no trade value rarely receive protection.
