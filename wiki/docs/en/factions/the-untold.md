# the Untold

The Untold are a secret group that worships Scarlet, led by Sera Vane. Most members hide deep in the city of Alybe, and Scarlet attacks them less often; a few members enter other groups as ordinary survivors.

## Formation

The core of the Untold came from a small pre-Blackout circle that secretly worshiped Scarlet. After the Blackout, Sera led her earliest followers deep into the city, evaded the Purge several times, and absorbed survivors seeking shelter.

The core stronghold has long been attacked less often, and the believers interpret this favorable treatment as the right to be spared by Scarlet. Sera decides who may approach the core stronghold; ordinary members usually know only the person who recruited them.

## Infiltration

Members sent to other groups keep their original names, work and make friends as usual, and send back intelligence on personnel and operations to the organization. Some members take part so that their families left deep in the city keep receiving shelter.

Scarlet uses the Untold to obtain intelligence on other groups, but still decides who is spared by her own population-screening criteria. The Untold's activities and strongholds are kept secret from outsiders. Infiltrators in other groups do not openly profess their faith.
