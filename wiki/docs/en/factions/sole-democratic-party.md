# Alybe Sole Democratic Party

The Alybe Sole Democratic Party is a radical party supported by lower-income laborers and led by [Elias Voss](../people/elias-voss.md). It advocates ending company rule and placing power in the hands of working residents, while treating internal dissent harshly. [Ruan Duo](../people/ruan-duo.md) handles dismantling and assault operations.
