# the Alybe Sole Democratic Party

The Alybe Sole Democratic Party is a radical party that was active on Alybe even before the Blackout, supported mainly by local low-level laborers and led by Elias Voss. The party advocates abolishing corporate governance and confiscating corporate assets, and establishing a city government controlled by "working residents."

## Pre-Blackout activities

The Sole Democratic Party long took part in residents' self-governance bodies, organizing negotiations over labor, housing, and identity. The party organization kept up its neighborhood ties through union dues and underground printing, building support among low-income laborers.

## Rule after the Blackout

After the Blackout, the Sole Democratic Party declared joint corporate governance defunct, occupied the old Residents' Assembly hall and the nearby districts, took in refugees, and formed a Civic Guard. The party organization distributed housing and food to low-level laborers, seized public facilities and stockpiles, and expanded its controlled area.

The territory enforces property registration, forced requisition, and grassroots denunciation. The party organization regards company managers, professional mercenaries, and some technical supervisors as beneficiaries of the old order; they must pass vetting before gaining full political rights. Those who refuse to hand over stockpiles face public trials, and opponents within the party are accused of stalling for the companies.

## The dismantling teams

After the Blackout, Ruan Duo led the workers and armed guards of the former industrial dismantling contractor teams into the Sole Democratic Party; the dismantling plants and their equipment came under party control, and the original work crews were assigned to the Civic Guard and supply-gathering teams. Ruan Duo is responsible for dismantling, equipment recovery, and assault operations, under Voss's leadership.

These teams retain the work-crew, quota, and collective-punishment system, with promotion based on task completion and seizures; combat personnel receive better food and lodging, while captives and the demoted are assigned dangerous labor, repairing requisitioned and seized equipment. Ruan Duo requires each crew to answer for its members' whereabouts and treats desertion as betrayal.

## Governance platform and conflicts

The Sole Democratic Party demands that the city's public facilities, taxation, and defense ultimately rest with a government elected by the residents. It competes with the Blue Sleeves, which advocates unified wartime command, over residents, warehouses, and governance. The party had a pre-Blackout partnership with the Neo Earth Joint Academy. Voss demands that the Academy hand all aid supplies to the party organization for distribution. This clashes with Liu Cen, who directly funds civilian evacuation.
