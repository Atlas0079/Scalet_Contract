# Survivor Groups

Survivor groups are the residents and armed organizations active on Alybe after the Blackout. On January 31, 2000 (30 days after the Blackout), most survivors were still hiding or on the move, gathering supplies and trying to join large groups. Only larger organizations could remain permanently stationed, maintaining guard, supply, and repair.

| Organization | Leader | Stronghold and activities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [the Blue Sleeves](blue-sleeves.md) | Marek Vik | Built around the remaining Valhalla garrison; wearing blue armbands; controlling the armory, repair shops, and adjacent residential areas; imposing military rule and preparing a counteroffensive. |
| [the Alybe Sole Democratic Party](sole-democratic-party.md) | Elias Voss | A radical party supported by low-level laborers, occupying the old Residents' Assembly hall and nearby districts, demanding the abolition of corporate governance and the adoption of requisition and political-rights vetting. Ruan Duo led the dismantling teams into the Civic Guard and supply-gathering teams. |
| [the Untold](the-untold.md) | Sera Vane | Most believers hide deep in the city and are attacked less often by Scarlet; a few members infiltrate other groups. |
| [the Black Stack](black-stack.md) | Xie Mi | Based on the port smuggling network, controlling freight yards and routes, collecting seizures centrally and distributing them by rank, running extortion, plunder, and escort. |

The Blue Sleeves and the Sole Democratic Party contend for city governance, and the Black Stack holds its strongholds under a fixed organization; the Untold's strongholds and member identities are kept secret from outsiders.

## Itinerant residents and scattered bandits

Itinerant squads spend the night in buildings, gather supplies, and look for large groups willing to take them in. Pre-Blackout corporate identity still shapes evacuation arrangements: company employees apply to their companies for retrieval, while local residents seek outside aid or arrange transport themselves.

Scattered bandits made up of stragglers, thugs, and hooligans operate through ad hoc gangs, robbery, and reselling loot, and disband easily after suffering losses. The Black Stack runs unified rank-based management and operates separately from these ad hoc gangs.

The Neo Earth Joint Academy provides supplies from its own funds, hires mercenaries to evacuate civilians, and arranges field tests of experimental items. The aid is handled by Liu Cen. Handover points change with operational arrangements.
