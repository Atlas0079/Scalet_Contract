# Survivor Groups

After the Blackout, most survivors hide or move between districts, seeking supplies and shelter. Large groups can hold fixed strongholds, maintain guards and supplies, and compete over roads and facilities.

| Group | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [Blue Sleeves](blue-sleeves.md) | Garrison remnants and military order |
| [Alybe Sole Democratic Party](sole-democratic-party.md) | Political organization of local laborers |
| [The Untold](the-untold.md) | A secret faith centered on Scarlet |
| [Black Stack](black-stack.md) | Underground trade and armed predation |

Scattered bandits also rob and resell supplies. Company personnel seek evacuation through their employers; local residents rely on outside aid or transport they arrange themselves.
