# Common Governance and Law (Draft)

The established background is joint company development and administration, residents with status independent of the companies, and a garrison belonging to Valhalla.

The division of public authority and the scope of residents’ self-governance remain to be determined. Company status and its effect on evacuation are described in [Residents and Identity](../society/residents.md).
