# Common Governance and Law (Draft)

The following is a draft system of common governance. The powers of its institutions and the details of its identity rules remain to be confirmed. The draft takes as its background the corporate joint development, residents' self-governance, and Valhalla garrison relationship already adopted in the main text.

## Joint development and governance

Under the Alybe Joint Development and Governance Compact, the companies share the costs of the port, infrastructure, and defense while keeping their own assets and personnel. The Joint Council decides public affairs. The Office of Public Administration handles day-to-day administration. Leviathan leads the negotiations on the strength of its funding and transport capacity. Amending the compact requires the support of the other signatories.

## Residents' self-governance

After the strike, the parties recognized residents' self-governance by amending the compact. Leviathan supported the compromise to restore production and weaken other companies' exclusive hold on labor. The Residents' Assembly manages the community, registration, and local policing, and holds the agreed public revenue. Changing self-governance powers requires the Assembly's consent.

## Identity and justice

Company identity covers the employed, the unemployed, and retirees. People may work and trade across companies. Changing affiliation requires settling prior obligations and obtaining the receiving party's recognition. Cross-company disputes and serious crimes are handled by the common courts. Enforcement depends on the parties providing funds, evidence, and personnel.

## Defense

Valhalla protects the colony under a long-term agreement. The public institutions set defense objectives and funding. The companies keep their own military chains of command. Other companies each maintain security for their own facilities. The orbital weapons are funded by all parties and operated by Valhalla.

Requisition and road control during a disaster are temporary authorizations. Headquarters has the right to order the garrison to withdraw, and bears the consequences of breaching the defense agreement. Existing contracts continue to govern post-disaster evacuation arrangements, loss determination, and accountability.
