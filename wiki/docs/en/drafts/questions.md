# Open Questions

The undecided institutions, technical conditions, and story arrangements are collected below.

| Scope | Items to be decided |
| --- | --- |
| Alybe's scale and operation | Actual width, depth, population, layer count, and number of bubbles; the functions of bubbles at different scales, transport and freight capacity, power supply, and environmental systems. The four layers and specific bubble count in the 3D study are structural illustrations only. |
| Surface conditions and domes | Surface temperature, pressure, and atmosphere; the form of the giant cold-shielding installations, environmental changes after dome failure, isolation of neighboring districts, and spacesuit protection requirements. |
| Common governance | The division of public authority and the scope of residents’ self-governance. |
| Personhood and trade | The legal personhood of androids and the boundaries of trade in disembodied brain tissue. |
| Opening commissions | The commissioning parties, objectives, hostile teams, and sequence of the Observer's first batch of missions. |
| Tiandiren commission | The specific conditions of the high-difficulty missions, the range of access rewards, and the content of the exclusive intelligence. |
| Scarlet's forces | How she commands robots and androids, and the relationship between robot control and SIA rules; the specific course of the screening, exploitation, and attacks suffered by each region. |
| Identity investigation | The specific content of the former colleagues, personal records, and disaster-recovery installation records, the order in which clues are delivered, and each faction's ending. |
| Cot weapons | The delivery mechanism, accuracy, warning, and interception, and the event that first demonstrates their deterrence. |
| Extraction and outfitting | The scope and timing of the phased extraction, and the specific rules for outfitting, recruitment, and low-resource recovery. |
| Observer view | How information from squad members and reconnaissance equipment is aggregated on satellite terminals, and how blind spots, latency, and interference are presented. |
| Psionics (second-tier setting) | Keep the treatment restrained, focusing on teleporting objects, blinking, manipulating objects at a distance, and disrupting perception and mental states. The mechanism, range, target conditions, duration, resistance, and consequences of load remain open. Eureka’s retained abilities after revival and the conditions for using psionics as disembodied brain tissue also remain open. |
| Armed Taoists and corpse animation | Review the concept, first defining local command execution versus SIA, eligible bodies, and skill sources; then determine range, capacity, animation and upkeep costs, termination behavior, and equipment use. |
