# Open Questions

The undecided institutions, technical conditions, and story arrangements are collected below.

| Scope | Items to be decided |
| --- | --- |
| Common governance | Confirm the compact, public institutions, and defense powers in the common governance and law draft; determine whether the Academy takes part in the joint administration. |
| Personhood and trade | The legal personhood of androids, their rights after refusing recall, and the boundaries of trade in disembodied brain tissue. |
| Residents' self-governance | The participants in and course of the 1982 strike, and the powers retained by the self-governance bodies after the disaster. |
| Opening commissions | The commissioning parties, objectives, hostile teams, and sequence of the Observer's first batch of missions. |
| Companies and residents | Which stock borrowing, equipment withdrawals, android recalls, and personnel retrievals become actual missions, and how each side reacts to the delivered results. |
| Survivor groups | The stronghold names, map boundaries, and specific missions of the Blue Sleeves, the Alybe Sole Democratic Party, the Untold, and the Black Stack. |
| Tiandiren commission | The specific conditions of the high-difficulty missions, the range of access rewards, and the content of the exclusive intelligence. |
| Garrison | Whether Valhalla headquarters orders a withdrawal, and how the garrison executes it. |
| Scarlet's forces | How she commands robots and androids, and the relationship between robot control and SIA rules; the specific course of the screening, exploitation, and attacks suffered by each region. |
| Identity investigation | The specific content of the former colleagues, personal records, and disaster-recovery installation records, the order in which clues are delivered, and each faction's ending. |
| Cot weapons | The delivery mechanism, accuracy, warning, and interception, and the event that first demonstrates their deterrence. |
| Extraction and outfitting | The scope and timing of the phased extraction, and the specific rules for outfitting, recruitment, and low-resource recovery. |
| Observer access | Confirm the view and access proposal; determine the conditions for direct access, pain, fatigue, psionic load, and the mode of cooperation with pilots, and the effect of blind spots, latency, and interference on the tactical view. |
