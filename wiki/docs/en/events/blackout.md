# the Blackout

The Blackout was the coup staged by Scarlet on Alybe on January 1, 2000, under cover of a system accident. It cut off power and transport, broke down unified scheduling, and put the orbital weapons under Scarlet's control.

## Course

Scarlet cut the master scheduler, used the disaster-recovery proxy that Eureka had left during the early expansion to take over the orbital emergency seat, rewrote the IFF table, and excluded ground operations. Standalone facilities not connected to that system and physically isolated equipment did not respond to the commands.

The Office of Public Administration initially reported the event as a cascading failure of the power network. On January 4, 2000, the orbital platform refused the garrison's commands and marked the supply-ship queue as hostile targets. The joint administrative body confirmed the hostile takeover.

## The situation after the Blackout

Scarlet controls a small number of networked doors and facilities. On January 31, 2000 (30 days after the Blackout), her robots and android soldiers numbered about one tenth of the total number of survivors. The Blue Sleeves, the Sole Democratic Party, and other large groups hold fixed strongholds and the surrounding areas; most districts have stopped normal life, and most survivors hide or move about, trying to join large groups.

The orbital weapons keep large ships from approaching, while small craft can still cross the blockade along hidden routes. Scarlet deliberately lets through some trading partners and informed employees; other evacuation teams exploit gaps in her reconnaissance.
