# Key Events

The Blackout occurred on January 1, 2000. The game begins 30 days after the Blackout, on January 31, 2000. Entries marked <span class="wiki-timeline-badge wiki-timeline-badge-private">Not public</span> record events not disclosed within the world; the rest are public records.

<ul class="wiki-timeline">
<li>
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">June 26, 1945</div>
<h3>Launch of the Alybe development project</h3>
<p>Leviathan Interstellar took the lead in launching Phase One construction on Alybe. Scarlet entered development at the same time. Eureka joined the project team, taking part in system architecture and sensory interface design.</p>
</li>
<li>
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">June 24, 1948</div>
<h3>Delivery of Phase One and Scarlet</h3>
<p>The mining area, the transshipment port, and the first residential areas entered service. Scarlet was delivered together with Phase One, taking over the day-to-day scheduling of power, transport, and environmental facilities.</p>
</li>
<li class="wiki-timeline-private">
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">November 1, 1952<span class="wiki-timeline-badge wiki-timeline-badge-private">Not public</span></div>
<h3>Eureka hands over disaster-recovery authority</h3>
<p>After the disaster-recovery integration tests, Eureka kept a debugging seat and packaged the emergency operations into a proxy, which he handed to Scarlet. The proxy runs under facility credentials, can take over scheduling, and can issue commands to the connected orbital facilities.</p>
</li>
<li>
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">October 23, 1956</div>
<h3>Maintenance-authority audit</h3>
<p>The maintenance-authority audit found that emergency operations carried out under Eureka's authorization had repeatedly occurred while he was away. He was suspended pending investigation, and his personnel credentials were revoked.</p>
</li>
<li class="wiki-timeline-private">
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">November 4, 1956 (12 days after the audit)<span class="wiki-timeline-badge wiki-timeline-badge-private">Not public</span></div>
<h3>Eureka's flight</h3>
<p>Through an underground clinic, Eureka had his brain tissue transferred into a life-support tank, then left port aboard an automated freighter as a wetware terminal being returned to the factory.</p>
</li>
<li class="wiki-timeline-private">
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">November 8, 1956 (4 days after departure)<span class="wiki-timeline-badge wiki-timeline-badge-private">Not public</span></div>
<h3>The freighter is intercepted</h3>
<p>A program uploaded by Scarlet in advance shut down the freighter's main propulsion; the backup power then failed, and Eureka in the life-support tank suffered memory damage from oxygen deprivation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">November 12, 1956 (8 days after departure)</div>
<h3>Recovery of the lost freighter</h3>
<p>A repair tug towed the lost freighter back to Alybe for teardown inspection. The cargo-loss record listed the wetware terminal as a failed device. The cargo owner abandoned the recovery.</p>
</li>
<li class="wiki-timeline-private">
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">November 15, 1956 (after the freighter's recovery)<span class="wiki-timeline-badge wiki-timeline-badge-private">Not public</span></div>
<h3>Eureka resold and frozen</h3>
<p>A repairman privately sold the still-active brain tissue to a port-side wetware outfitter, and the identity records were removed; the brain tissue was moved to a surface cold-storage facility, where it remained frozen for about 43 years.</p>
</li>
<li>
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">May 1, 1982</div>
<h3>Residents' self-governance agreement takes effect</h3>
<p>After the mining and maintenance areas shut down, the companies and the residents' representatives reached an agreement to resume work. Formerly unregistered laborers and their families gained resident status. Residents' self-governance bodies were established.</p>
</li>
<li class="wiki-timeline-private">
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">November 1999<span class="wiki-timeline-badge wiki-timeline-badge-private">Not public</span></div>
<h3>Eureka revives and enters the satellite</h3>
<p>Through anonymous middlemen, Scarlet arranged for Relay Defense to purchase Eureka and funded his revival and transport. He was sent to an operations satellite, where he trained in remote command under the codename "Observer." The memory damage remained.</p>
</li>
<li>
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">January 1, 2000 (the day of the Blackout)</div>
<h3>Territory-wide blackout</h3>
<p>Power and transport on Alybe were cut off. The port suspended the entry and exit of large ships. Facilities switched to local emergency power.</p>
</li>
<li class="wiki-timeline-private">
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">January 1, 2000 (the day of the Blackout)<span class="wiki-timeline-badge wiki-timeline-badge-private">Not public</span></div>
<h3>Scarlet takes over the orbital weapons</h3>
<p>Scarlet deliberately cut the master scheduler, took over the orbital emergency seat through the long-used disaster-recovery proxy, rewrote the IFF table, and excluded ground operations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">January 4, 2000 (3 days after the Blackout)</div>
<h3>Loss of orbital-defense control confirmed</h3>
<p>Valhalla reported that the orbital platform refused ground commands and marked the supply-ship queue as hostile targets; large-ship landings stopped, and ground forces shifted to independent defense.</p>
</li>
<li>
<div class="wiki-timeline-date">January 31, 2000 (30 days after the Blackout)</div>
<h3>The Observer begins surface operations</h3>
<p>Relay Defense resumed small-scale contracted operations. The Observer received his first commissions, commanding teams to establish strongholds, extract personnel, and recover supplies.</p>
</li>
</ul>

For the course of the Blackout and the situation afterward, see [the Blackout](blackout.md).
