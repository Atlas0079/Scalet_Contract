# Residents and Identity

Alybe's population is divided between company-affiliated personnel and local residents with independent status. Company status resembles nationality and carries attached treatment, administration, and resettlement arrangements; residing in the colony does not change one's affiliation.

## Pre-disaster composition

Before the Blackout, company employees and their families made up about three-tenths of the population and generally held higher social standing than local residents. Company status carried strong personal subordination: moving in or out was difficult, and ties with the company remained even after unemployment.

Most local residents descend from illegal laborers brought in during the colony’s development. After the 1982 strike, residents gained formal status independent of the companies and established self-governance bodies.

## Post-disaster resettlement

After the Blackout, resettlement of company personnel is handled by their companies. Local residents evacuate through the self-governance bodies, aid from Neo Earth Joint Academy, or transport they organize themselves. Most survivors move between city districts, trying to join large groups that can hold strongholds over the long term. Rank-and-file laborers broadly support the Alybe Sole Democratic Party.
