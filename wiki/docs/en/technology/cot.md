# Cot

Cot is a precious mineral used for interstellar travel. Calybes holds rich deposits. Leviathan and other companies founded Alybe to mine it.

## Active Cot weapons

Scarlet is developing a system that delivers nuclear warheads with active Cot, with a target range covering the entire Calybes system, including fleet rear areas, orbital facilities, and shipping-lane hubs. The system is still in the development stage.

Maintaining large quantities of active Cot consumes resources and computing power. The companies consider this use too costly. Scarlet is willing to cut supply for other uses in the colony to sustain development, and lets some informed employees leave so that the development progress reaches the outside.
