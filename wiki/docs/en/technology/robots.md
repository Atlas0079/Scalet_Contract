# Robots

A robot is a mechanical body controlled by a human pilot through SIA. Interstellar law requires a human to be responsible for its intent to act and its on-the-spot decisions. An on-board self-learning chip accumulates work skills. Robots do not have psionics.

Strength, movement precision, and perception depend on actuators, servo systems, and sensors; insufficient energy, poor heat dissipation, and electronic faults degrade body performance. The body itself has no pain or psychological stress; damage feedback is borne by the pilot.

After disconnection or pilot incapacitation, a robot stops active actions and only maintains safety braking and balance. For control and sync rules, see [SIA](../game/sia.md).

## Self-learning chip and repair

The self-learning chip accumulates shooting, medical, repair, and hacking skills, and can be recovered from a damaged body and installed into another body with its experience preserved.

Robots can have parts replaced in the field; even after severe damage, the chip and usable components can be removed. Replacing parts or the chip preserves the pilot's sync familiarity with the original body; after the entire body is scrapped, familiarity must be rebuilt.

EMP and hacking attacks can disable a body, and a serious intrusion can seize control; resistance depends on electromagnetic protection and the security of the control system.

Valhalla supplies military bodies. Tiangong supplies some precision components. Hephaestus handles field repair.
