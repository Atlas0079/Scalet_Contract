# Androids

Androids are humanoid life forms capable of carrying self-consciousness, composed of synthetic tissue, muscle fibers, and a circulatory system, with body strength far above humans and agility higher than conventional robots; they rely on synthetic blood to stay active.

Synthetic blood reserves, metabolic load, and circulatory condition affect physical ability; tissue stability and circulatory efficiency affect injury recovery. Repairing synthetic tissue costs more than conventional mechanical repair, and severe damage usually requires sending the body back to its matching Body Refit Center.

Golem provides the bodies and maintenance specifications. Different models use their corresponding synthetic blood formulas. Ashvin manufactures some synthetic blood and consumables on contract. Tiangong supplies some manufacturing and inspection equipment.

## Autonomy and takeover

An autonomous android judges and bears stress and pain on its own; its experience belongs to its current consciousness. Consciousness can only be switched on or off at base, and when switched off it retains existing memories and skills.

A human pilot can take over a body with its consciousness off through SIA, using the pilot's experience and the muscle memory accumulated by the body's own consciousness. The dormant consciousness takes no part in decisions. The psychological burden and damage feedback are borne by the pilot.

After disconnection, remote active actions stop. The consciousness state is preserved until handled during outfitting at base. Androids cannot serve as SIA pilots and have no psionics. Carrying a consciousness of human origin does not change these bodily limits.

## Autonomous androids after the Blackout

After the Blackout, some autonomous androids joined large survivor groups, taking on work or guard duty, refusing Golem's recall, and having companions go to refit centers to collect consumables for them. Golem still records these collections in the body files.
