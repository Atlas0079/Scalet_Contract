# Psionics

Psionics are anomalous abilities humans can wield to change the position of objects, exert force on them, and disrupt human perception and mental states. Their effects include teleporting objects, blinking, manipulating objects at a distance, inducing blindness, and causing mental loss of control.

Greater skill primarily improves precision, while increases in the scale of power are comparatively limited. A beginner can bring a large rock from a place entirely unknown to them, but can only make it appear at a random position in front of them. A psionic master can precisely control where and how the effect is applied, even breaking an enemy’s neck at a distance. The precise application of limited force can be lethal.

Psionic performance depends on individual potential, training, and psychological state. Use accumulates psionic load. Humans wield psionics themselves; robot and android bodies do not possess this ability.

The underlying mechanism, range, targeting conditions, duration, and consequences of psionic load remain to be determined. See [Open Questions](../drafts/questions.md).
