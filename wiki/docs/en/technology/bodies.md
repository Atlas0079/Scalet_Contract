# The Three Combat Body Types

Humans, robots, and androids have different body structures, consciousness carriers, and maintenance conditions. Humans can act with their own bodies and can also serve as SIA pilots. Robots are controlled by pilots. Androids have two modes: autonomous activity and SIA takeover.

| Item | Human | Robot | Android |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Body structure | Biological body. | Mechanical structure, actuators, and sensors. | Synthetic tissue, muscle fibers, and a circulatory system. |
| Control | Own consciousness. | Controlled by a human pilot through SIA. | Own consciousness, or taken over by a human pilot after the consciousness is switched off at base. |
| Work skills | Experience in the individual's own consciousness. | Mainly the self-learning chip, combined with pilot experience. | Uses its own skills when autonomous; under SIA takeover, pilot experience dominates, assisted by muscle memory. |
| Main features | Has psionic potential and can serve as an SIA pilot. | Parts are replaceable, convenient for field repair and recovery. | Body strength far above humans; agility higher than conventional robots. |
| Operational burden | Fatigue, stress, pain, and psionic load. | Energy, heat dissipation, electronic faults, and the pilot's state. | Synthetic blood, metabolism, circulation, and the current controller's state. |
| Maintenance and losses | Requires treatment and rest; elites are costly to train, and revival is difficult after true death. | Recovering the chip preserves skills; replacing parts restores body function. | Depends on dedicated consumables and outfitting; repair after injury is expensive. |
| Psionics | Wielded through the human themselves. | None. | None. |

Pilots control remote bodies through SIA. The higher the sync rate, the better the control performance and the stronger the damage feedback. For skill ratios and pairing familiarity rules, see [SIA](../game/sia.md).

For the three body types individually, see [Humans](humans.md), [Robots](robots.md), and [Androids](androids.md).
