# Leviathan Interstellar

Leviathan Interstellar is Alybe's largest investor and mining operator, holding the Cot mining rights, freight contracts, and port warehousing. The other companies take on its construction and procurement orders, and ship their products off-world through its shipping business.

Leviathan began with long-haul fleets and colonial investment; its branches across the star systems report to a single headquarters. It funded the construction of Alybe's mining areas and transshipment port, contracting out equipment, engineering, and defense to the other companies, while the colony's public affairs are jointly administered by the companies.

## Port account disputes

The port department confirms ownership of goods by warehouse receipt, while the mining department settles output by delivery record. The Blackout interrupted the reconciliation between the two sets of records, leaving the same shipments booked as delivered at the mine but not received at the port; the departments hire mercenaries to locate the original records and the stranded containers, each demanding that they be returned to its own office.

## Deals after the Blackout

Some managers fund Valhalla's counteroffensive, while others trade privately with Scarlet for passage of designated personnel and assets; the passage arrangements cover only the agreed voyages and cargo, and other departments of the same company must still negotiate on their own.
