# Companies and Institutions

Alybe was jointly developed and is jointly administered by companies. Its institutions cover mining, shipping, arms, industrial equipment, precision manufacturing, androids, healthcare, and education and research. After the Blackout, companies hire mercenaries to extract personnel and recover assets.

| Institution | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [Leviathan Interstellar](leviathan.md) | Mining, colonial investment, and shipping |
| [Valhalla Heavy Industries](valhalla.md) | Conventional arms and the colonial garrison |
| [Hephaestus Interstellar](hephaestus.md) | Industrial equipment and field maintenance |
| [Tiangong Group](tiangong.md) | Precision manufacturing and construction |
| [Ogun Group](ogun.md) | Emerging arms producer and experimental equipment |
| [Golem Life Consortium](golem.md) | Android development and body refitting |
| [Ashvin Life Consortium](ashvin.md) | Healthcare, pharmaceuticals, and life-support consumables |
| [Neo Earth Joint Academy](new-earth-academy.md) | Education, research, and civilian aid |

[Mercenaries](mercenaries.md) provide combat and extraction services.
