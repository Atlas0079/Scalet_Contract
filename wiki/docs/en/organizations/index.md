# Companies and Institutions

Alybe is jointly developed and administered by the companies. Leviathan Interstellar led the founding of the colony, contracting out equipment, engineering, and defense to the other companies. After the Blackout, the corporations dispatch surface teams aboard the mercenary companies' small spacecraft to extract personnel and recover assets.

- [Leviathan Interstellar](leviathan.md) — Alybe's largest investor and mining operator, holding the Cot mining rights, freight contracts, and port warehousing.
- [Valhalla Heavy Industries](valhalla.md) — the main arms supplier and the company the garrison belongs to, producing conventional firearms, protective gear, and military robots.
- [Hephaestus Interstellar](hephaestus.md) — industrial equipment supplier and maintenance contractor, producing generators, pump units, and hauling equipment.
- [Tiangong Group](tiangong.md) — builds industrial facilities and produces precision machine tools, sensors, and servo components.
- [Ogun Group](ogun.md) — a corporate group that entered the arms market late, trialing weapon power components, special ammunition, and electronic jamming equipment on Alybe.
- [Golem Life Consortium](golem.md) — develops and supplies androids and operates Alybe's Body Refit Center.
- [Ashvin Life Consortium](ashvin.md) — operates Alybe's corporate hospitals and medical supply, and manufactures android synthetic blood on contract.
- [Neo Earth Joint Academy](new-earth-academy.md) — a research institution conducting environmental and medical research and funding civilian evacuation.

For the institutional details of how the companies share public affairs, see [Common Governance and Law (Draft)](../drafts/governance.md). For the mercenary companies hired after the Blackout to carry out extraction and recovery, see [Mercenaries](mercenaries.md).
