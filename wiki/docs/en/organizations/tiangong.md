# Tiangong Group

Tiangong Group builds industrial facilities and produces precision machine tools, sensors, and servo components. Valhalla uses its actuator components to build military bodies. Golem and Ogun use its manufacturing and inspection equipment.

Tiangong became a group by merging machine-tool, sensor, and engineering companies; the subsidiaries keep their own product lines and operating accounts, while the group headquarters holds the controlling stakes, investment allocation, and key personnel appointments.

## Certification and calibration

Certification data for precision equipment is kept by factory serial number; after key components are replaced, the equipment must be recalibrated, and engineers sign the delivery records according to the results — a false signature costs the signer the delivery qualification. Hephaestus's temporary repairs may first restore pump units or transport equipment to operation, but precision systems that have not been recalibrated cannot be delivered at their original accuracy.

## Engineers after the Blackout

The Blackout idled some production lines; the surviving engineers carried out certification data and key spare parts, tested stripped components in the original laboratories, regrouped identical-model parts taken from different workshops according to the measurements, and sent them to equipment still in operation.

The group has arranged to move out some inspection equipment, part of which is still in use by large survivor groups to service water pumps and medical equipment; the resident engineers list it as equipment in continuous use and demand that replacements be brought in before it is shipped out.
