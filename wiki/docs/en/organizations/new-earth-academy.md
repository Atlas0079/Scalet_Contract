# Neo Earth Joint Academy

Neo Earth Joint Academy is a research institution conducting environmental and medical research on Alybe and funding civilian evacuation, jointly established by multiple teaching and research institutions; the members send field teams and jointly allocate station funding and research facilities, while outside project grants are used project by project.

Before the disaster, the academy's field stations kept environmental samples, case records, and long-term survey records. Researchers made long-term visits into the mining and maintenance areas, reaching laborer families without corporate medical coverage. The academy purchased drugs and medical consumables from Ashvin.

## Aid after the Blackout

After the Blackout, the academy uses its own funds to buy aid supplies and hires mercenaries to carry out civilian extraction and evacuation; Dr. Liu Cen is in charge of local aid, signing the extraction contracts directly and arranging the delivery of supplies and the transport of people, with the handover points and temporary assembly sites changing from operation to operation.

At the same time, the academy arranges field tests of experimental items, providing mercenaries with experimental hemostatic materials, portable water purifiers, and environmental detection equipment, and requires them to bring back usage records. Liu Cen prefers to put funding into equipment that can expand the scale of the evacuation.

The academy has a pre-disaster cooperative relationship with the Alybe Sole Democratic Party. Party leader Voss demands that aid supplies be handed over to the party organization for unified distribution. This conflicts with the academy's arrangement of directly organizing civilian evacuation.
