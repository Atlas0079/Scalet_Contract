# Non-Vacuum Intelligence

Non-Vacuum Intelligence is a mercenary company running on-site reconnaissance, intelligence trading, and personnel extraction. On Alybe it buys and sells facility entrances, personnel locations, and small-spacecraft routes. Its informants include port clerks, maintenance workers, and facility administrators.

The company sells intelligence by delivery scope; the same item can be sold to multiple clients. Exclusive items cost extra, with the contract setting a retention period after which the company may sell again. Facility-entrance and route intelligence is marked with the time of its last verification; clients who want updated records must commission further reconnaissance.

Informants' identities are kept separate from the sold content, and the company delivers only the information. When buying intelligence from informants, the company separately agrees on extraction terms: providing information itself carries no evacuation quota; extraction contracts list the designated persons, and adding family members or companions requires rearranging capacity and renegotiating the price.

After the Blackout, Non-Vacuum Intelligence locates missing persons and arranges secret evacuations, contesting targets and routes with Relay Defense and the Galatians. It also trades route updates for escort from the two companies. Deals cover only the agreed voyages.
