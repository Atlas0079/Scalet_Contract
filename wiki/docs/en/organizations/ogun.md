# Ogun Group

Ogun Group is a corporate group that entered the arms market late, trialing weapon power components, special ammunition, and electronic jamming equipment on Alybe. It supplies corporate security and mercenaries with small equipment batches, collects usage records to improve its products, and contests Valhalla's orders.

The group consists of weapons-development, ammunition, and electronic-equipment companies held by the same parent, with the subsidiaries each running their own development projects. Headquarters expands its technology through acquisitions and investment and pursues arms orders centrally; production uses Tiangong's manufacturing and inspection equipment.

## Trial batches

Trial batches use different components and control settings; the equipment ships with batch markings, and even identical-looking prototypes must be maintained according to their own documentation. Some contracts require clients to return the equipment and usage data, and failures occurring in use are likewise entered in the trial records.

Ogun once brought in illegal laborers; these laborers and their descendants make up part of Alybe's local population.

## Sample depots after the Blackout

After the Blackout, equipment from overrun sample depots and test ranges fell into the hands of armed groups. The group offers separate bounties for the recovery of the prototypes and their recording modules; returning only the recording module still earns the corresponding payment. When mercenaries keep the weapons in service, the group still obtains field data on that equipment.
