# Hephaestus Interstellar

Hephaestus Interstellar is Alybe's industrial equipment supplier and maintenance contractor, producing generators, pump units, and hauling equipment. Many of its models share consumables and fasteners, so field crews can strip machines and swap parts between them.

Hephaestus grew out of cross-system engineering contracting; its workshops and engineering teams in the various locations are all directly-owned branches, and equipment assets and contracts are centrally managed by headquarters. The company once brought in illegal laborers; some of these laborers and their descendants later obtained local resident status, and the maintenance workshops still recruit from these communities.

## Division of work with Tiangong

Tiangong supplies some precision components, while Hephaestus handles the matching equipment and day-to-day maintenance. Crews record temporary replacements and jumper wiring on the service tags kept beside the equipment, and Tiangong engineers recalibrate the precision systems according to these records.

## Crews after the Blackout

After the Blackout, surviving crews strip decommissioned equipment and restore local power, pumping, and transport. Some crews join large survivor groups and maintain equipment inside their controlled areas, with the groups providing fuel and guards; the rest carry their tools and move with the teams. Repairs are settled in parts, fuel, or labor hours; the service tags travel with the removed components, recording which machine they were taken from and what modifications they have undergone.
