# Relay Defense

Relay Defense is a mercenary company focused on remote-robot combat, and the organization the Observer belongs to at the start. Its scattered business nodes take orders independently; most of its human pilots work part-time, and combat teams assemble by mission.

The nodes handle settlement and arrange repair facilities, while bodies are held by the node, the contracted team, or the pilot. Before deployment, both sides agree on damage compensation and the division of recovered goods, and the pilot roster and the body inventory are registered separately. The sync familiarity a pilot has accumulated with their original body is unaffected by a change of body; a new pairing still has to be broken in.

The nodes share performance records: injured pilots are given contracts according to their current control capability, and personnel who went missing must restore contact and have their identities verified before they can take new orders. When a node ceases operations, other nodes may form new teams to take over its contracts; the bodies and funds held by the original node must be recovered separately.

After the Blackout, the company ships bodies aboard small spacecraft and takes on garrison, escort, and armed-recovery work. On January 31, 2000 (30 days after the Blackout), the Observer received the first batch of surface contracts from their node and began directing a team to establish a stronghold.
