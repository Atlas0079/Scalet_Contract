# Mercenary Organizations

Three mercenary companies operate near Calybes. After the Blackout, companies use their small spacecraft to deploy surface teams, extract personnel, and recover assets.

- [Relay Defense](relay-defense.md): Remote robotic combat contracting
- [Non-Vacuum Intelligence](non-vacuum-intelligence.md): Field reconnaissance, intelligence trading, and covert extraction
- [Galatians](galatians.md): Human combat contracting
