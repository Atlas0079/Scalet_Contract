# Mercenaries

Relay Defense, Non-Vacuum Intelligence, and the Galatians are the three mercenary companies near Calybes. After the Blackout, the corporations dispatch surface teams aboard their small spacecraft to extract personnel and recover assets.

The three companies fight over routes, strongholds, and recovery targets. Non-Vacuum Intelligence sells route updates to the other two, and also trades intelligence for escort on designated voyages; specific commissions are settled separately, and teams in cooperation still take assignments from their own companies and clients.

- [Relay Defense](relay-defense.md) — fights primarily with remote robots; the Observer's starting company.
- [Non-Vacuum Intelligence](non-vacuum-intelligence.md) — runs on-site reconnaissance, intelligence trading, and personnel extraction.
- [The Galatians](galatians.md) — works primarily with human combatants, taking on assaults, stronghold contests, and armed escorts.
