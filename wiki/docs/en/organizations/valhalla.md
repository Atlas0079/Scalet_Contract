# Valhalla Heavy Industries

Valhalla Heavy Industries is Alybe's main arms supplier and the company the garrison belongs to, producing conventional firearms, protective gear, and military robots. Mature models are supplied on a long-term basis; generations of equipment share ammunition and components, and customers can obtain maintenance documentation.

Valhalla began in heavy machinery and armor manufacturing, and kept the "Heavy Industries" name after entering the general arms market. Its manufacturing divisions and company military are run by a single headquarters, which also arranges the garrison's reinforcements and follow-up supplies; Ogun contests its arms orders with small trial batches.

## The garrison after the Blackout

After the Blackout, the garrison lost command of the orbital weapons and its stable supply. The original command went silent. Logistics Major Marek Vik gathered garrison troops who had lost their units, facility guards, and logistics personnel, incorporated civilian volunteers, and formed the Blue Sleeves. Using the original armories, repair plants, and the adjoining residential areas as strongholds, the Blue Sleeves restored the supply lines and prepared a counteroffensive.
