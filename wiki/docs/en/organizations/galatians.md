# The Galatians

The Galatians are a mercenary company whose fighters are primarily human, taking on assaults, stronghold contests, and armed escorts; teams assemble around squad leaders with a combat record, and members earn their standing through shared combat and loot.

The company's offices take outside contracts, while each squad recruits and divides the loot on its own. Squad leaders expand the contested objectives during operations on their own initiative, then demand extra payment from clients on the strength of the additional loot; recovered goods the client declines to buy are sold by the team to other buyers.

The Galatians buy facility entrances and target locations from Non-Vacuum Intelligence and contest strongholds and recovered goods with Relay Defense. After taking a warehouse, squads often take on the next contract together with the guards and haulers still on site, delivering the now-garrisoned location directly to the new client.
