# Ashvin Life Consortium

Ashvin Life Consortium operates Alybe's corporate hospitals and medical supply, producing common drugs and medical consumables, and manufacturing android synthetic blood to Golem's technical specifications. The companies reserve services and stock for their own populations through long-term medical contracts.

Ashvin was jointly founded by hospital networks, pharmaceutical plants, and medical insurance institutions; the members keep their own facilities and staff, and member representatives jointly manage centralized procurement, cross-region supply, and external medical contracts. Valhalla buys field medical supplies from it, and Neo Earth Joint Academy buys drugs for aid supplies and civilian evacuation.

## Hospitals after the Blackout

After the Blackout, the hospitals take in large numbers of wounded not covered by the contracts while retaining the medical reserves purchased by corporate clients; some clients have lost contact with the hospitals, and the hospitals still in operation cannot on their own transfer the reserved stock to the academy.

Hospital pharmacists hand drugs nearing expiry to the aid transport teams organized by the academy, registering them as contracted stock to be replaced. The academy handles the transport and usage records, then restores the original stock through purchases. This arrangement puts the drugs to use before the cold chain fails, while leaving a restocking obligation still to be fulfilled.
