# Golem Life Consortium

Golem Life Consortium develops and supplies androids and operates Alybe's Body Refit Center, providing synthetic tissue, circulation components, and specialized maintenance equipment; clients who buy a body must still obtain consumables matched to its model.

Golem was jointly founded by institutions and companies holding technologies in synthetic tissue, consciousness carriers, and body refitting; the members keep their own technical assets and jointly manage production, technology licensing, and outfitting business. The repair of severe tissue damage, and the switching between autonomous consciousness and SIA takeover, are both handled by the matched outfitting facilities.

Ashvin manufactures some synthetic blood and consumables to Golem's technical specifications; different models use the corresponding formulas, and the refit center issues them according to the bodies' maintenance records. Resident hospitals can treat some circulation damage, but severe tissue damage must still be sent back to the refit center.

## The recall after the Blackout

After the Blackout, the consortium recovers refrigerated samples, maintenance records, and lost bodies, demanding recall on the strength of maintenance contracts and equipment ownership. Some autonomous androids who joined large survivor groups and took on work or guard duty refuse to leave; their companions go to the refit center to exchange for consumables. The center attaches these pickup records to the body files and keeps tracing the whereabouts of those subject to recall.
