# Iron Eye

The Iron Eye setting wiki collects the world, characters, organizations, events, and gameplay rules of the Alybe colony on Calybes.

On January 1, 2000, Alybe lost unified coordination in the Blackout. The infrastructure AI [Scarlet](people/scarlet.md) controls the orbital weapons and part of the networked facilities; large groups such as the Blue Sleeves and the Sole Democratic Party hold city districts, while most residents hide or move with squads. The game opens 30 days after the Blackout (January 31, 2000), with the player taking the role of [Observer](people/observer.md), a remote command officer of Relay Defense.
