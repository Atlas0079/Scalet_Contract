# SIA

SIA (Somatic Integration Architecture) is the control architecture connecting human pilots and remote bodies, used for robots and androids whose consciousness is switched off; the pilot supplies the intent to act and on-the-spot judgment, the body executes the movements, and damage is fed back to the pilot as pain.

## Control relationship

SIA pilots are humans; their consciousness always remains attached to their own biological body while they control a remote body from base. One pilot directly controls one body at a time, and each body accepts input from one effective controller at a time.

Robots perform active actions through SIA; an android must switch off its own consciousness at base before being connected, and restoring autonomous consciousness also takes place at base. During a takeover, actions are decided by the pilot, and the body's muscle memory takes part in executing the movements.

Effective control requires a pilot, an authorized link, and an available body: link quality affects sync maintenance, communication delay affects reaction speed, and the pilot's stress and pain affect operating performance.

On disconnection, pilot incapacitation, or the sync rate dropping to zero, the body stops active actions and maintains the necessary safety braking and balance. Changing the pilot or the body ends the current control and establishes a new connection; unfinished operations are interrupted when control switches.

## Sync rate

Sync rate indicates the state of the connection between a pilot and the current body. A higher sync rate improves control performance while intensifying the pain carried by damage. A lower sync rate weakens feedback and reduces control effectiveness.

A pilot accumulates sync familiarity with each body separately; actual control raises the sync ceiling that this pairing can sustain. Repairing, replacing mechanical parts, or replacing the chip preserves the existing pairing familiarity; after the entire body is scrapped, familiarity must be rebuilt with the new body.

The current sync rate recovers gradually after the connection is established, and is affected by link quality, damage, and drugs. After the connection is interrupted, the current sync state ends. Pairing familiarity accumulated over the long term is preserved.

## Skill combination

Robots and androids combine work skills in different ways. The ratios below apply to shooting, medical, repair, and hacking skills. SIA control capability comes from the pilot personally.

| Takeover target | Pilot skill | Body-side skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Robot | 30%. | 70% from the self-learning chip. |
| Android | 70%. | 30% from the muscle memory of the body's own consciousness. |

Corresponding work experience is credited to the pilot and to the chip or body memory in the same proportions. When the body-side carrier of experience is missing or unreadable, that portion of the skill contribution is zero, and the share of experience is not transferred to the pilot; the sync rate applies to the combined control performance.

Chip recovery preserves its skill experience; an android's muscle memory accumulates with its current own consciousness; a pilot separately accumulates their own SIA experience through control. The strength, agility, and sensory conditions of the remote body are determined by its own structure.

## Damage feedback and state

The base feedback intensity of body damage, multiplied by the sync rate at the moment of injury, forms the pain borne by the pilot: at 100% sync rate the pilot bears the full feedback, and at 50% half of it. Feedback is settled according to the connection at the time the damage occurred; a later disconnection or lowering of the sync rate preserves the pain already incurred.

The pilot's will affects performance after enduring pain. A robot body itself has no pain or stress. A dormant android consciousness likewise bears no psychological load during a takeover. Remote operation is affected by the pilot's current state.

A pilot may use drugs on themselves that adjust the sync rate. The drug's effect is bounded by the current sync ceiling between the pilot and the body. Drugs change the performance of the current connection. The long-term sync ceiling is accumulated through actual control.

While a pilot is using an item, remote active input is paused. The connection and damage feedback remain in effect. After the operation is finished, the pilot may issue action orders again.

## Law and business

Interstellar law requires robots to be controlled by humans through SIA. Relay Defense organizes its contracting business around robots and part-time human pilots. Golem provides facilities for android outfitting and control-state switching.
