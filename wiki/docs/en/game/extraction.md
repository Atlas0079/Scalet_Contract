# Game: Extraction Cycle

The Extraction Cycle consists of supply gathering, extraction, off-mission outfitting, and the next deployment. The player arranges for squad members to carry supplies off the field and may extract in batches.

## Search and assignment

Searching reveals supplies in containers and other sources; when finished, the game pauses automatically and opens the assignment interface. Assignment only reserves the pickup — supplies remain in place, and the designated carrier must reach the site to complete the transfer; the searcher and the carrier may be different members.

## Extraction and losses

Supplies enter the off-mission warehouse after extracting together with their actual carriers; when a battle ends, equipment and supplies not carried out are lost, and fallen squad members are lost permanently. Contract completion and delivery are recorded separately.

Some members may extract early, keeping the personnel and gains already carried out while the remaining members continue to operate on the field; losses suffered later by the team remaining on the field do not deduct supplies already extracted.

## Off-mission outfitting

After an operation ends, the squad refits with the resources brought back. Personnel losses are replenished through recruitment. The treatment, repair, and consumable needs of each body type are covered in [the three combat body types](../technology/bodies.md).
