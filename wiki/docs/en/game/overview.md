# Game: Iron Eye

Iron Eye is a top-down sci-fi tactical shooter built around squad command, supply gathering, and extraction. The player plays as the Observer on an operations satellite, taking contracts on Alybe, establishing a base, and investigating their own identity.

Battles progress in real time; the player may pause freely to arrange orders, and direct commands take priority over automatic engagement. Squads gather supplies and carry them out through extraction; the gains fund off-mission outfitting and the next deployment.

Squads are composed of humans, robots, and androids, each with different sources of skills and different maintenance needs. SIA handles the direct control of remote bodies by human pilots.

## World background

On January 1, 2000, Alybe, a colony of Calybes, was struck by the Blackout. Scarlet controls the orbital weapons and some facilities. Large groups hold fixed strongholds. Most survivors hide or keep moving. The game opens 30 days after the Blackout, on January 31, 2000.

The Observer starts affiliated with Relay Defense; their true identity is the amnesiac Eureka. The story includes faction contracts and an identity investigation.
