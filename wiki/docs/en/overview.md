# Story Overview

Calybes is a remote mining planet with rich deposits of [Cot](technology/cot.md). Leviathan Interstellar led the founding of [Alybe](places/alybe.md), the only colony on Calybes, jointly developed and jointly administered by the companies. Alybe's primary business is Cot mining and freight transshipment for nearby port planets.

Alybe's population is divided between company-affiliated personnel and local residents with independent status. Before the Blackout, company employees and their families made up about three-tenths of the population. Most local residents descend from illegal laborers brought in by Hephaestus Interstellar and Ogun Group. After the 1982 strike, residents gained formal status independent of the companies. See [Residents and Identity](society/residents.md).

On January 1, 2000, Scarlet launched [the Blackout](events/blackout.md), cutting power and transport and seizing the orbital weapons. The game opens 30 days after the Blackout, on January 31, 2000. Alybe is still under the Purge and orbital blockade, and most districts have ceased normal life. Scarlet controls the orbital weapons and a small number of networked facilities; her forces are insufficient to occupy the whole city. Large groups such as the Blue Sleeves and the Sole Democratic Party maintain guards, fuel, repairs, and transport, while fighting continues over the roads between strongholds and scattered districts. Most survivors hide inside buildings or move with squads, seeking large groups willing to take them in. Evacuation depends on small spacecraft: companies hire crews to take out their employees, Neo Earth Joint Academy funds and hires mercenaries to evacuate civilians, and the remaining residents arrange their own transport.
