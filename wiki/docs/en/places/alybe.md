# Alybe

Alybe is the only colony on Calybes, founded under the lead of Leviathan Interstellar and jointly developed and jointly administered by the companies. Its primary business is Cot mining and freight transshipment for nearby port planets.

## Urban structure

Alybe consists of "bubbles" of widely varying sizes and functions, joined by passageways. Roads form an irregular network within each layer, while a small number of elevators connect different layers. The bubbles are unevenly distributed, with their numbers, spacing, and connecting routes varying by district. The number and distribution of elevators also vary.

The city's depth approaches its breadth. Its overall form narrows downward like an inverted pyramid with its tip removed, retaining some width at the deepest level. The uppermost bubbles lie on the surface, where giant installations shield them from the planet's extreme cold.

## Establishment

On June 26, 1945, Leviathan Interstellar led the start of Alybe's phase-one construction. On June 24, 1948, the mining district, the transshipment port, and the first residential areas entered service. Scarlet was delivered together with the phase-one works and took over the daily dispatch of power, transport, and environmental facilities.

## Joint governance

Alybe is jointly developed and jointly administered by the companies. After the 1982 strike, residents gained formal status independent of the companies, and the residents' self-governance bodies were established. The institutional details of joint governance remain in draft form; see [Common Governance and Law (draft)](../drafts/governance.md).

## Alybe after the Blackout

After the Blackout, some surface bubbles have shattered domes. Humans and androids both need spacesuits to enter these areas.

On January 31, 2000 (30 days after the Blackout), Alybe is still under the Purge and orbital blockade, and most districts have ceased normal life. Residents hide inside buildings or move with squads, gathering supplies and seeking large groups willing to take them in; open activity is concentrated in a few fixed strongholds.

Homes, offices, and stations still bear traces of pre-disaster life; some production lines keep running, and the districts hit by the Purge are now uninhabited. The lights and broadcasts of some buildings still switch on according to their original schedules.

Scarlet controls the orbital weapons and a small number of networked facilities; her forces are insufficient to occupy the whole city, and the mines and factories still depend on residents working. She temporarily keeps some districts where people work and deals with company executives to maintain production and delay outside intervention.

The Blue Sleeves, the Sole Democratic Party, and other large groups can maintain guards, fuel, repairs, and transport, are still drawing down pre-disaster stockpiles; fighting continues over the roads between strongholds and scattered districts.

The orbital blockade makes evacuation dependent on small spacecraft: companies hire crews to take out employees and recover assets; Neo Earth Joint Academy funds supplies and hires mercenaries to evacuate civilians; the remaining residents arrange their own transport or seek acceptance by large groups.
