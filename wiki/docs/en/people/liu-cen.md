# Liu Cen

Liu Cen is a doctor of environmental medicine and the Neo Earth Joint Academy's aid chief on Calybes. She has long investigated occupational diseases among Alybe's workers and knows many of the families now scattered.

After the Blackout, Liu Cen organized evacuation funding and purchased aid supplies, contracting mercenaries directly for civilian pickup. The supply handover and personnel assembly points change with each operation's arrangements.

She also handles field testing of experimental items, preferring to put funds into equipment that can expand the scale of evacuations, and requires mercenaries to bring back usage records. The Academy's test items include experimental hemostatic materials, portable water-purification devices, and environmental detection equipment.

Liu Cen had a pre-Blackout cooperative relationship with the Sole Democratic Party. After Voss demanded that the Academy's aid supplies be handed to the party organization for distribution, the two sides came into conflict.
