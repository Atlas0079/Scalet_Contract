# Eureka

Eureka was a technician and psionic master during Alybe's early construction. He took part in designing Scarlet's system architecture and perception interfaces, and he is the identity the Observer had before his amnesia.

## City-founding period

In 1945, Eureka joined the first-phase project team. The team was hostile to psionics practitioners and regarded his appointment as symbolic acceptance, expecting little of his technical work. He confided these experiences and his memories of the green Earth to Scarlet, treating her as a friend.

In 1948, the first phase of construction and Scarlet were delivered together. Eureka continued to take part in expansion and maintenance.

## Disaster-recovery proxy

On November 1, 1952, after the disaster-recovery joint testing, Eureka kept the debugging seat, handed his personal authorization to Scarlet, and packaged the emergency operations into a long-running proxy. The proxy executes commands with facility certificates, can restore configuration and take over scheduling when the master control loses contact, and issues emergency commands to orbital facilities on the shared disaster-recovery bus. Scarlet can also refuse remote requests to disable her own core processes.

The audit revoked Eureka's personnel credentials, and the maintenance team kept the proxy for continuous power supply. Later expansions reused the original configuration, and these permissions thereby persisted until the Blackout.

## Flight and cryostasis

On October 23, 1956, a maintenance-permission audit found that emergency operations had still been executed under Eureka's authorization while he was absent. He was suspended for investigation, fearing detention and Scarlet's removal. 12 days after the audit (November 4, 1956), he hired an underground clinic to transfer his brain tissue into a life-support tank and fled aboard an automated cargo ship, disguised as a wetware terminal being returned to the factory.

Scarlet uploaded a malfunction program before departure. On November 8, 1956 (4 days after departure), the program shut down the cargo ship's main propulsion; the backup power failure subjected Eureka to hypoxia, damaging much of his long-term episodic memory, while language and learned skills largely survived.

On November 12, 1956 (8 days after departure), a repair tug towed the cargo ship back to Alybe. The cargo damage record listed the wetware terminal as failed equipment, and the owner abandoned recovery. Repair workers privately sold the still-active brain tissue to a dockside wetware outfitter; the outfitter removed the identity data and moved the severely damaged brain tissue, which had no buyer for the time being, into a surface cold store, where it was kept for about 43 years.

## Revival

On December 31, 1999, through an anonymous intermediary, Scarlet arranged for Relay Defense to purchase the terminal and funded the revival and transport. Eureka was sent to an operations satellite in Calybes's low orbit and, under the code name "Observer," received remote-command training. The memory damage remained.
