# Ruan Duo

Ruan Duo heads dismantling and assault operations for the Alybe Sole Democratic Party, under Elias Voss's leadership. He was formerly the chief foreman of an industrial dismantling contractor crew.

He rose from high-risk work to foreman, familiar with equipment, blasting, and large-structure dismantling, and once took part in suppressing work stoppages among his own workers. After the Blackout, the contractor crew's management fled. Ruan Duo led the remaining workers and armed guards to join the Sole Democratic Party, placed the dismantling plant and equipment under the party organization's control, and folded the existing work shifts into the Civic Guard and supply-collection teams.

Ruan Duo personally takes part in difficult dismantling and assault operations, and decides promotions in the teams under him by missions completed and captures. He insists on collective punishment for the whole shift, treating desertion as a betrayal of the other members. Captives and the demoted are assigned dangerous labor.
