# Tiandiren

Tiandiren is a mysterious figure able to control many of Alybe's devices under Scarlet's rule. He holds special door controls, authority to provide experimental items, and important exclusive intelligence.

Outside, only a few company executives in contact with the Observer know the name. Among the surface factions, only Marek Vik and Sera Vane know of Tiandiren's existence. The other members of their respective organizations do not.

Tiandiren contacts the Observer one-way, only at moments of his own choosing, and never appears in person. The Observer cannot call him, and there is no stronghold he could visit.

He offers highly difficult commissions, paying in "permissions": access permissions to designated facilities and special door controls, as well as permissions to collect or use experimental items. He also holds important exclusive intelligence that no other client can provide.
