# Elias Voss

Elias Voss is the leader of the Alybe Sole Democratic Party. He comes from a labor family in the mining district and enjoys broad support among lower-tier workers.

As a young man, Voss worked as a transport worker and a shift bookkeeper, and was long banned from several major factory districts for organizing work stoppages and opposing the company's withholding of wages. Afterward, he kept his political organization alive on union dues and underground printing, and took part in residents' self-governance and identity negotiations.

After the Blackout, Voss calls for completely seizing urban governance from the companies, confiscating company assets, and establishing a government ruled by the "working residents." He supports allocating housing and food to lower-tier workers, while implementing forced requisitioning, grassroots denunciation, and political-rights vetting, listing company managers, professional mercenaries, and some technical supervisors as vetting targets.

After the Blackout, Ruan Duo led the dismantling crews' workers and armed guards into the party organization, accepted Voss's leadership, and took on dismantling, equipment recovery, and assault operations.

Voss accuses opponents within the party of being the companies' stalling agents, and demands that the Academy hand all aid supplies over to the party organization. This puts him in conflict with Liu Cen, with whom he had cooperated before the Blackout.
