# Xie Mi

Xie Mi is the leader of the Black Stack, nicknamed "the Treasurer." He was formerly a tally clerk at a port freight company, long forging cargo damage records for smugglers, and then entered the underground trade.

Xie Mi excels at keeping track of warehouses, buyers, and the debts of all parties. After the Blackout, he used grain and transport to absorb the guard teams, and set up a system of centralized collection and distribution by rank, forbidding members from embezzling captures or killing, on their own, anyone who could be ransomed.

He keeps goods flowing by honoring some of his deals, and also detains people who refuse to pay fees and seizes other groups' goods. For residents with no trade value, he rarely provides protection.
