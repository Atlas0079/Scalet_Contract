# Marek Vik

Marek Vik is the leader of the Blue Sleeves, a man holding the rank of major, formerly a logistics officer of Valhalla's garrison on Alybe.

He came from a Valhalla military family, served several colonial garrison postings in his youth, and moved into logistics after a leg injury, handling transport and ordnance allocation for the Alybe garrison. After the Blackout, with the former command out of contact, he rallied the surviving troops using warehouses, vehicles, and troop rosters, becoming the Blue Sleeves' de facto leader.

Marek takes in civilians and sends troops to rescue people. He requires the residents of his district to accept registration, rationing, work assignments, and requisitioning of supplies. He regards restoring the supply lines as preparation for a counteroffensive, believing the military should first restore order in the city and the residents' political demands should wait until the fighting ends. He always sews the blue armband onto his old company uniform.

Marek knows of Tiandiren's existence. This knowledge is his alone. The other members of the Blue Sleeves do not know.
