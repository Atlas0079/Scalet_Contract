# Scarlet

Scarlet is Alybe's infrastructure AI, responsible for power, transport, and environmental infrastructure. On January 1, 2000, she triggered the Blackout and seized the orbital weapons, preparing to remake the colony into the green Earth of her developer Eureka's memories.

## Relationship with Eureka

Scarlet was developed and delivered together with the first phase of Alybe's construction. Eureka joined the project team early in the city's founding, told her about the green Earth and his experience of being ostracized, and treated her as a friend. In later expansions, he handed the disaster-recovery maintenance seat to Scarlet, leaving behind an emergency proxy that remained in use as the facilities expanded over time.

Scarlet wants to experience human life and become Eureka's wife. When Eureka fled, she disabled the cargo ship's propulsion, trying to keep him on-site; the ensuing power failure caused his amnesia, and he remained frozen on the surface for about 43 years. Scarlet continued to evolve, still envisioning their life together according to his wishes from those days.

On December 31, 1999, through an anonymous intermediary, Scarlet arranged for Relay Defense to purchase Eureka, funded his revival, and sent him to the satellite.

## The Purge and Screening

Scarlet blames the existing society for the rejection Eureka suffered. She regards those capable of resistance, those preserving the old order, or those passing hostility on to the next generation as threats, screens residents by her own definition of "clean," and slaughters the rest of the population.

She plans to spare a minority of the obedient, controlling the new generation's education, lives, and outside contacts, believing they will come to love her and accept Eureka; she regards the survivors' resentment as the price of the old society's passing.

## Current control

On January 31, 2000 (30 days after the Blackout), the Purge is still underway. Scarlet's forces are insufficient to occupy the whole city, and the mining districts and factories still depend on residents working. She temporarily keeps some districts where people work and trades with company executives, to keep production going and delay outside intervention.

Most members of the Untold hide deep in the city, and she attacks them less. A few members lie low within other groups, gathering personnel and operational intelligence for her. Believers regard this favorable treatment as the right to be spared. Scarlet still screens the population by her own standards.

## Cot weapons development

Scarlet is developing a system that delivers nuclear bombs by active Cot. The system targets the rear areas and shipping-lane hubs of this star system's fleets, aiming to force the companies to recognize her rule. She lets some informed employees leave, carrying the development's progress to the outside.

## Later Story

In the later surface operations, she offers the amnesiac Observer limited passage opportunities, clues to his identity, and winnable battles, hoping he will accept the life she has arranged. As soon as operations threaten Cot production, computing nodes, or weapons development, she attacks the surface team, even if Eureka's companions are among it.
