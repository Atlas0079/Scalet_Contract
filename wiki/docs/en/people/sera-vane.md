# Sera Vane

Sera Vane is the leader of the Untold. She once worked the night shift of Alybe's public broadcast.

Before the Blackout, Sera was long exposed to Scarlet-generated notices, emergency broadcasts, and withdrawn messages, and gradually interpreted their discrepancies as Scarlet's guidance to a chosen few. After the Blackout, she led her earliest followers deep into the city, evaded the Purge several times, and established her authority within the group.

Sera holds the hideouts and contact methods of the core members, and assigns a few members into other groups. She reads Scarlet's relative restraint toward believer strongholds as "the right to be spared," and decides who may approach the core strongholds. Ordinary members usually know only their own recruiter.

Sera knows of Tiandiren's existence. This knowledge is hers alone. The other members of the Untold do not know.
