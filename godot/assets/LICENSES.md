## CRT shader reference

`presentation/maintenance/crt.gdshader` adapts **Realistic CRT shader** by **c64cosmin**, published as CC0 at https://godotshaders.com/shader/realistic-crt-shader/ . The original credits c64cosmin for the grille and integration, pend00 for warp/vignette, Timothy Lottes for scanlines and AHOPNESS for the port. This project adapts viewport texture input, luminance calibration, strength controls, curve-aware mouse mapping and low-amplitude noise/interference/rolling-line effects. The reference page’s images and other depicted assets are not incorporated.

## Cabin enclosure decoration

The enclosure panels, ribs, beveled terminal frames, contact blocks, washers/hex fasteners, rubber-lined straps, stepped cable glands/boots, annular pipe clamps, supported cable-tray routes and rounded cooling-loop geometry are project additions. The narrowed layout retains six batched material meshes. Steel and painted structures retain original 1K diffuse, roughness, OpenGL normal and metallic maps from Metal Plate 02 by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, CC0: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02 . Its four equal horizontal regions depict aged charcoal rubber cable jacket, dirty tan woven cable insulation, oxidized copper/brass, and scratched dark phenolic/backing material. `shell_detail.gdshader` uses periodic cylindrical sampling on cable surfaces and metre-scale triplanar sampling on hardware. Fine relief and roughness/metallic response are constructed material estimates. The source image is retained at `/Users/hewei/.codex/generated_images/01a0fce7-0803-74e2-a217-8bda00ed7ace/exec-86cc1732-f33c-41f4-873c-679602ca07e0.png`; exact prompt and native inspection tooling remain local under `.art-preview-local/cabin-textured-walls/`. No external photograph is incorporated in this atlas.

The camera variant `environment/security_camera_01/articulated.gltf` reuses the original Poly Haven CC0 geometry and textures. Connected source components are assigned to a fixed wall base/support (6,527 triangles) and a moving head (7,042 body triangles plus the original 2 glass triangles). `articulated_indices.bin` contains only the partitioned index lists; source vertex/normal/UV buffers, material assignments and triangle coverage are unchanged. This corrects the rotating attachment while retaining source vertices, indices, maps and the fixed mounting plate.
